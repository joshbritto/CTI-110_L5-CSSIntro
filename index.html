The Simpsons is an American Animated Sitcom starring the Simpsons Family in the fictional town of Springfield. It caricatures Society, Western Culture, and Television.

  • Homer is the Dad of the family, he is very lazy and very unintelligent.
  • Marge is the Mom of the family, she loves her family and does whatever it takes to keep them in check.
  • Bart is the oldest son of the Simpson family, he likes to do pranks and get into trouble.
  • Lisa is the middle daughter of the Simpson family, she like to get good grades and playing the saxophone.
  • Maggie is the baby of the Simpson family, she loves her pacifier and she cannot get away from it.
